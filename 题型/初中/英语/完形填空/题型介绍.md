# 题型介绍

## 长什么样

**一篇短文挖若干空，每个空给若干选项，选一个。语境型重点是词义、搭配与上下文，也可能有语法或逻辑连接空。**

完形填空首先是词汇和语篇理解题。多数空的选项是不同词，主要靠词义、搭配与上下文；少量空还须查语法，不能说所有完形都不考形式。

以广东中考为例：一篇短文，挖 10 个空，每题四选一，共 10 分。

容易混的四个：

| 题型 | 区别 |
|---|---|
| 语法选择 | 选项是同一个实词的不同形式（`silent / silence / silently`）或虚词，考语法 |
| 完形填空 | 主要选不同实词，也可混有逻辑或语法空 |
| 选词填空 | 给一个词框，从框里选词，不是每题自带选项 |
| 语法填空 | 不给选项，按提示词自己写形式（高中） |

**完形填空和语法选择最容易混：看选项是不是同一个词的不同形式。**

## 示例

### 2026 年广东省中考英语｜完形填空

> 请通读下面的短文，掌握其大意，从每小题所给的四个选项中选出一个最佳答案。
> 来源：2026 年广东省中考英语 · 原卷文字整理，**未加入答案和解析**，只用于看结构

A team of high school students in a coastal city have developed a storm-shelter app. It helps people find shelters (庇护所) quickly during bad weather.

The team started by ____1____ information of the local shelters from the government website. Then they used AI tools to turn the information into an app. This app ____2____ all the local shelters on the map. When a storm comes, the app can guide a user ____3____ to the nearest shelter. It also gives safety instructions to help its users make best ____4____. The local emergency management organization noticed the app and put it on its official website.

Mr. Green, their science teacher, thought these students did a good job and he felt very ____5____. “The students put what they have learned into practice. They are using ____6____ to solve real problems. And they try their best to give back to the community. I am sure they will be ____7____ adults when they grow up,” he said.

So far, the app has got thousands of downloads. The young developers never expected that their app would be so ____8____. They were truly excited to be able to create something helpful for a large community. “Every year, many storms hit our city. Local people suffer a lot. We are glad that people can ____9____ our app,” Eric, a team member, said. The team are now working to ____10____ the app. They will add information of more shelters so that the app can be used across the country.
