# 题型介绍

## 长什么样

**左栏若干条信息，右栏一组选项（广告卡片、说明等），为每条信息配一个选项。选项多于信息，有多余项。**

以本工作区收录的样例为例：左栏是五种植物的介绍，右栏是花店的七张广告卡片，为每种植物匹配合适的卡片，其中两项多余。

配对看的是**这条信息和选项说的是不是同一事物、同一用途或同一诉求**，不是语法形式。

规则按题面确认，不从“配对阅读”这个标签反推：

- 选项能否重复使用、每项用几次，按题面；
- 多余项有几个、是否与话题相关，按题面。

题面没写清时标为**待核实**，向教师或原出处确认，不靠“剩下的一项”推答案。

## 和谁像

| 题型 | 区别 |
|---|---|
| 选词填空 | 给一个词框，把词填进短文的空，不是整条信息配整张卡片 |
| 阅读理解（句子还原） | 把句子放回短文里的空，恢复语篇连贯 |
| 还原句子（初中） | 短文挖空，所有空共用一个完整句子池，按当前学段处理 |

## 示例

### 广东中考英语 · 配对阅读

> 左栏是对五种植物的介绍，右栏是花店的七张广告卡片。请为每种植物匹配合适的广告卡片。选项中有两项为多余选项。
>
> 来源：广东中考英语 · 原卷文字整理，**未加入答案和解析**，只用于看结构

**左栏（小题）**

1. The Venus Flytrap is amazing. When an insect like a fly falls into it, it closes its leaves within half a second. Then it turns the insect into its food.
2. The Sensitive Plant is known for its interesting leaves. When you touch it, its leaves close up. In Chinese, it is called “Shy Grass.”
3. The Air Plant is famous for its ability to grow without soil. It lives well in indoor environments.
4. Aloe Vera has thick leaves. The juice from its leaves can be used to treat burns that are not serious.
5. Sunflowers have big flowers. They like sunlight and require watering every day. Their bright yellow color makes them a good choice for gardens.

**右栏（选项池）**

| | 选项 |
|---|---|
| A | You will not see flies in your house again if you take it home. |
| B | The best place for it is the kitchen. You can use it for first aid if you get your hands burnt while cooking. |
| C | Would you like an unusual plant? Take this plant. It doesn’t need soil or bright sunlight. |
| D | Love big flowers? This plant is the right choice for you. It will make your garden lively and pretty. |
| E | This is Lucky Bamboo. It provides a sense of positive energy. Get it and you’ll be lucky. |
| F | Want a plant that can “communicate” with you? Try touching this plant and see how “shy” it will become. |
| G | This plant can flower all year round. Its small purple flowers will surely bring life to your room. |
