# 题型介绍

## 长什么样

**一篇短文挖若干空，给一个词框，从框里选词填入；题目可能要求用正确形式。**

作答前先看题型说明，它决定怎么选、怎么排除：

| 规则 | 常见写法 |
|---|---|
| 变形 | `使用正确形式` / `change the form` |
| 一词几次 | `每词仅用一次` / `use each word once` |
| 多余词 | `有两词多余` / `extra words` |
| 每空几词 | `每空一词` / `one word for each blank` |

**规则不同，做法就不同。** 说明要求原词时不能擅自变形；说明要求正确形式时，也只做有依据的变化，不自由派生到另一词性。

容易混的三个：

| 题型 | 区别 |
|---|---|
| 语法选择 | 每题自带三个选项，选一个，考语法 |
| 完形填空 | 每题选项是不同实词，考词义 |
| 语法填空 | 不给词框，按提示词自己写形式 |

**选词填空、语法选择是中考题型（初中），语法填空是高考题型（高中）。**

## 示例

### 2026 年广东省中考英语 · 短文填空

> 请从方框内选择适当的词并用其正确形式填空，使文章完整连贯。注意每空一词，每词仅用一次，有两词为多余选项。
>
> 来源：2026 年广东省中考英语 · 原卷文字整理，**未加入答案和解析**。

Yang Ming is a 26-year-old designer. He once studied and worked in Shanghai. There he developed his designing skills. Two years ago, he ____1____ to his hometown. With the help of the government, Yang started ____2____ own business.

Yang's hometown is known for its fishing ____3____. This gives him lots of creative business ____4____. He mixes local cultural characteristics into his products. So far, he has developed more than 30 products. Among them, the most successful one is a refrigerator magnet. It sells much ____5____ than the others in gift shops. The magnet has the shape of a traditional fishing boat. It reminds the tourists ____6____ their travel experience in Yang's hometown.

"Cultural products are not about simply printing traditional symbols on products," Yang said. "The key is to find a natural connection between tradition ____7____ modern life."

Yang is not ____8____. Across China, a growing number of young designers are turning to traditional local culture for inspirations. As their products become popular abroad, more foreigners will ____9____ a better understanding of Chinese culture. "Chinese traditional culture ____10____ be seen by the world as long as we find the right way to present it," Yang said.
