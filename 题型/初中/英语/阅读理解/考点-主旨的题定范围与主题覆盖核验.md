## 讲解

### 判据

mainly问指定段或整篇，先划范围再提核心对象和作者重点。例子、支撑因素不能代主旨，首尾句需全文或全段验证。

### 流程

1. 花灯题限Paragraph3，段首theme不同选择，例创意设计，段尾rich themes，概括丰富主题；其余历史、传承者、科技未为此段重点。
2. Jenny题问story想告诉什么，合作搭档克服缺技能并练习胜，末friend help总结，A为全篇。
3. B自信与D练习确参与，但属合作成功条件，不最能覆盖找朋友与互助主线；C行动胜言太泛且未直接支持。
4. 用主要内容逐块验证候选，范围过小、过宽或未证分别说明，不以词出现即选。
5. 原励志anything是夸张鼓励，不把文章主题推出任何困难只要朋友便必胜。



## 例题

### 原例题 EN-READ-E14

# 例1 (2024 山东滨州中考节选)

( Para. 3 ) What's more, the theme of the lantern can be chosen in different ways. "Anything

that can be designed on a lantern can make a wonderful show, and everyone is encouraged to turn their ideas into real works," said Zhang Fanglai, a leading researcher at the Colorful Lantern Museum of China. And the rich themes of Zigong lanterns show the spirit of creativity and warmth from the bottom of the local people's heart.

According to the passage, what's Paragraph 3 mainly about?

A. The rich themes of Zigong lanterns.   
B. The long history of Zigong lanterns.   
C. The excellent inheritors of Zigong lanterns.   
D. The modern technologies of Zigong lanterns.

**原答案**：A。

**原解析（原文）**

主题句在段首。分析结构可知“What's more, the theme of the lantern can be chosen in different ways.”为本段主题句，本段主要讲自贡花灯丰富的主题。故选A。

**完整解析（依据原解析展开）**

段首theme可多种方式选择，引述任何能设计的想法都成作品，尾rich themes支持A丰富主题。B历史、C传承者、D科技未是本段论述对象；专家引述是解释创意主题不是全文变成专家传记。题问Paragraph3不擅概括未给全文。

### 原例题 EN-READ-E15

# 例2 (2024 山东日照中考)

Jenny, a fifth-grader, is considered one of the most popular girls in her class. She is admired not just for her looks, but for her talent for dance. Last year, she entered the school's dance competition and won first place, with her whole class cheering her on. Since then, she has been the center of attention. This year, however, there was a change—the competition required dancing in pairs.

Jenny's classmates held high hopes for her to win again this year. However, Jenny was uncertain about the result. She wanted to compete, but she had never danced with a partner before. As she was turned down by the boys in her class due to their lack (缺乏) of dance skills, Jenny began to feel hesitant (犹豫) about entering the competition.

She finally turned to her close friend Carl, whom she had known since first grade. Lacking confidence in his dancing abilities, Carl was hesitant at first. But Jenny insisted. He finally agreed to join her. Jenny was happy. However, she was still worried because she knew her class was counting on her to win. But the challenge was greater this time, as Carl was not a skilled dancer. With the competition two months away, only time would tell if they could succeed.

With strong will and enough practice, Jenny believed they could improve their chances of winning. Jenny and Carl practiced a lot and worked together. At the competition, they danced amazingly well and won!

When friends help each other, they can achieve anything.

What does the story mainly want to tell us?

A. Friends help succeed.   
B.Believe in yourself.   
C. Actions speak louder.   
D. Practice makes perfect.

**原答案**：A。

**原解析（原文）**

主题句在文章末尾。文章主要讲述了 Jenny 和 Carl 通过互相帮助,最终在舞蹈比赛中取得了胜利的故事。最后一段得出结论,说明朋友之间的互相帮助可以帮助我们成功,A 项概括了文章的主题。故选 A。

**完整解析（依据原解析展开）**

Jenny需搭档，Carl起初不自信后合作练习取胜，结尾明确friends help each other，A朋友助成功统摄。B自信、D熟能生巧是支持细节，不能代核心合作；C行动胜语言过宽未为明确主旨。文中achieve anything是故事鼓励话，不当现实无条件成功保证。
