# 七年级上册（人教版 2024）

> 英语的教材目录**不止标题**：重点是每单元的**话题 / 句型 / 语法**（单元考准备、写作训练要靠它们）。
> 段落里的英文是教材原词，直接用来对齐单元。

## Starter Unit 1 Hello!　p.1

- **话题**：打招呼、开始对话
- **句型**：Hi. / Hello. / Good morning. / How are you? / Nice to meet you. / What's your name? / How do you spell your name?
- **语音**：26 个字母
- **听 / 说**：Greet each other
- **读 / 写**：Start a conversation
- **项目**：学会用词典

## Starter Unit 2 Keep Tidy!　p.7

- **话题**：物品与摆放
- **句型**：What do you have in your schoolbag? / What colour is the cap? / What colour are the rulers? / Some books are in / on / under the box.
- **语音**：a, e, i, o, u（1）
- **听 / 说**：Ask about things you have
- **读 / 写**：Describe things around you
- **项目**：猜猜它是什么

## Starter Unit 3 Welcome!　p.13

- **话题**：院子与农场里的东西
- **句型**：What's this / that? / What are these / those? / How many rabbits do they have?
- **语音**：a, e, i, o, u（2）
- **听 / 说**：Talk about things in a yard
- **读 / 写**：Describe a farm
- **项目**：设计自己的农场

## Unit 1 You and Me　p.19

- **话题**：交新朋友（How do we make new friends?）
- **句型**：介绍自己与他人；What do we need to know about a new friend?
- **语法**：一般现在时（be 动词）；主格代词 I / you / he / she / it / we / they；缩写形式
- **语音**：/iː/ /ɪ/ /e/ /æ/
- **写作**：回复一条交友帖
- **项目**：做自己的个人档案

## Unit 2 We're Family!　p.27

- **话题**：家庭（What does family mean to you?）
- **句型**：What is your family like? / What do you like about your family?
- **语法**：一般现在时（do）；名词所有格 's
- **语音**：/ɜː/ /ə/ /ʌ/ /ɑː/；单词重音（1）
- **写作**：用一张照片介绍你的家人
- **项目**：做自己的家谱

## Unit 3 My School　p.35

- **话题**：学校（What do you like about your school?）
- **句型**：What is your school like? / What fun things do you do at school?
- **语法**：There be 结构；方位介词（in front of / behind / between / next to / across from）
- **语音**：/ɔː/ /ɒ/ /uː/ /ʊ/；单词重音（2）
- **写作**：给朋友写邮件介绍你的学校
- **项目**：带你参观我的学校

## Unit 4 My Favourite Subject　p.43

- **话题**：学科（Why do we learn different subjects?）
- **句型**：Why do you like this subject? / What can you learn from different subjects?
- **语法**：连词 and / but / because
- **语音**：/eɪ/ /aɪ/ /ɔɪ/ /əʊ/ /aʊ/；/ɪə/ /eə/ /ʊə/；连读
- **写作**：写一条关于自己学科的帖子
- **项目**：设计一周课表

## Unit 5 Fun Clubs　p.51

- **话题**：社团（Why do we join school clubs?）
- **句型**：How do you choose a school club? / What can you learn in a school club?
- **语法**：情态动词 can（表能力）
- **语音**：/p/ /b/ /t/ /d/ /k/ /g/ /f/ /v/；不完全爆破
- **写作**：给社团负责人写邮件申请加入
- **项目**：办自己的社团

## Unit 6 A Day in the Life　p.59

- **话题**：日常作息（How can you make good use of your time?）
- **句型**：How do you spend your school day? / How different are people's daily routines?
- **语法**：时间表达；Wh- 问句（what time / when / what）
- **语音**：/s/ /z/；/ʃ/ /ʒ/；/θ/ /ð/；/tʃ/ /dʒ/；句子重音
- **写作**：写自己的学校生活
- **项目**：采访别人的日常作息

## Unit 7 Happy Birthday!　p.67

- **话题**：生日（Why do we celebrate birthdays?）
- **句型**：How do we celebrate birthdays? / How do you make your birthday meaningful?
- **语法**：序数词；Wh- 问句复习
- **语音**：/h/ /r/ /l/ /m/ /n/ /ŋ/ /w/ /j/；语调
- **写作**：写一条关于自己怎么过生日的帖子
- **项目**：一起过生日
