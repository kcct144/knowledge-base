## 讲解

### 判据

给定五事件排顺序，e和c先定事件集两端，b/d需辨首次与鞭后仍拒，不能缩范围后直接宣称全部唯一。

### 流程

1. 将a警告、b鞭打、c奖励、d拒服从、e到桥逐条定位，标行为对象。
2. 给定集e最先、c最后，筛掉B的b开头和D的d开头，仍比较A与C。
3. C在警告前已奖励，违反回家收束，所以排；A中e→b→d→a→c按鞭后再次拒绝可成立。
4. 明示实际首次拒绝发生b前，d原未指定次数；若指首次实际是e→d→b→a→c但选项未列。
5. 保留原A及原详解，不静默删前次停止或篡改d；首尾仅筛选，中间相邻都要核。
6. 回读原全文，叙事开头赴城并非这五事件之一，事件集起点不冒充全文起点。



## 例题

### 原例题 EN-READ-E03

# 例 (2024 江苏扬州中考)

One day in autumn Squire Gordon, my master, went on a trip into town. John, my caretaker, went with him. I pulled the cart. The weather was rainy and windy, but we travelled happily.

Gordon's business took a long time. We didn't leave until late afternoon. The wind was then much higher. My master said to John, "I have never been out in such a storm!" Mr Gordon and John could not see well in the rain.

We came to a wooden bridge. It was very nearly dark. We could just see that the water was over the middle of the bridge. But as that happened sometimes when the floods were out, my master did not stop. The moment my feet touched the first part of the bridge, I felt sure there was something wrong. I dare(敢于) not go forward, and I made a dead stop. "Go on, Beauty," said my master, and he gave me a touch with the whip(鞭子), but I dare not move. He gave me a sharp cut and I jumped, but I dare not go forward.

"There's something wrong, sir," said John, and he got out and tried to lead me forward. "Come on, Beauty." But I knew that the bridge was not safe.

Just then the man on the other side saw us.

"Hey! Halloo! Stop!" he cried.

“What’s the matter?” shouted my master.

"The bridge is broken in the middle, and part of it is carried away. If you go onto it, you'll be into the river."

“Thank you for saving us, Beauty!” said John. He gently turned me round to the right-hand road by the riverside. It grew darker and darker, stiller and stiller.

When we returned home the Squire told Mrs Gordon, “Black Beauty saved our lives. He knew the bridge was dangerous.”

John took me to the stable. Oh! What a good supper he gave me that night! What a nice bed of straw! I was glad of it, for I was tired.

—Adapted from Black Beauty

Which of the following is the correct order of the story?

a. The man warned them not to cross the broken bridge.

b. The master touched Black Beauty with the whip.

c. Black Beauty was well rewarded for his decision.

d. Black Beauty refused to obey his master at the bridge.

e. They three came to a wooden bridge, ready to cross the river.

A.ebdac

B.bdaec

C.edcab

D.dbeca

**原答案**：A。

**原解析（原文）**

根据第三段第一句“We came to a wooden bridge.”可知,他们来到一座木桥前,准备过河,e为故事的开端;根据最后一段中的“What a good supper he gave me that night! What a nice bed of straw!”可知,主人回家后犒劳了黑骏马,c为故事的结尾,所以答案为A。进一步验证答案,根据文章可知,故事顺序为:他们准备过河时,黑骏马意识到危险不敢向前;主人用鞭子抽打它;黑骏马

仍然拒绝前进;一个男人警告他们不要过桥;黑骏马回家后得到了犒劳。故选 A。

**完整解析（依据原解析展开）**

先定位选项事件而非文章全开头：e来到木桥在这些事件中最先，c回家犒劳最后，A/C才同有e起点，但C把c犒劳放在a桥断警告之前，排除。B把b鞭打排在e到桥前，D把d拒绝放e到桥前且以a结尾，均不合事件位置；A.ebdac最好符合原预设。

中间需明示d有重复：马触桥先停止，主人鞭打后又but I dare not move及but I dare not go forward，仍拒绝。原d仅说at bridge refused obey，没有限定“第一次”；按鞭打后的再次拒绝，e→b→d→a→c可成立，故保留原A。但若把d理解第一次拒绝，则e→d→b→a→c也是故事中确有的顺序而原选项没列，这一表述边界不能省。原详解也先说马察觉危险，再鞭打，然后“仍然拒绝”。不把第一次拒绝静默删掉来强行证明先b；首尾定位只是排除起点，仍需验证全部相邻事件与重复发生范围。

## 易错

重复事件需要阶段标记；本原题d有表述边界，不能用所谓“唯一顺序”盖过它。
