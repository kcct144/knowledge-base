## 讲解

### 判据

目标词后For example给旧T恤做包，同段先谈recycling再说upcycle不同，需要把例对到目标而非相邻词。

### 流程

1. 标refashion及其主语object，确认当前讲upcycle的动作。
2. 例make a bag out of an old T-shirt：旧物改成新用途，C概括实际改变。
3. A分解是前面的recycling说明，与different对比不合；B.collect只收集，D想点子未实际加工，均未解释例动作。
4. 把C代入simply refashion与same/even better condition，整体符合改造而非破碎重制。
5. 构词re提示重做可辅助，但无需据词形发明材料处理流程，清楚例子优先。



## 例题

### 原例题 EN-READ-E10

# 例（2024 广东广州中考节选）

(Para. 2) Is it the same as recycling? Recycling usually takes old things, like paper and glass, and breaks them down to make a new product. When you upcycle an object, you are probably doing something different. You can simply refashion it. For example, you might make a bag out of an old T-shirt. Also, the upcycled object is usually in the same, or even better condition.

What does the underlined word “refashion” mean in paragraph 2?

A. Break down useless things.

B.Collect used things.

C. Create things out of old ones.

D. Come up with good ideas.

**原答案**：C。

**原解析（原文）**

由画线词后的 For example 可知,后面的内容是对画线词的举例说明。根据“you might make a bag out of an old T-shirt”可知,refashion 的意思是“用旧的东西创造新的东西”,故选 C。

**完整解析（依据原解析展开）**

For example用旧T-shirt做bag示例，C旧物创造新物概括refashion。A.break down为前句recycling分解路径，当前强调different；B.collect只是收集非改造，D.come up ideas只是想法非实际改物。回读upcycle对比和例，不能取相邻recycling说明套到目标词。

解释要看指向哪个词，段里同时出现两概念不能互移定义。
