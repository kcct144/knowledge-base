## 讲解

### 判据

同篇1—2完成句子、3—5简答，第5明确两个不同方面。逐题识别输出，别用整篇同一抄法。

### 流程

1. 题1已有learn a lot from，缺来源；将原animals and plants in nature接回，不重复from或只留animals。
2. 题2缺spread out宾语，原their arms and legs；题干sugar gliders复数，保留their，两部位缺一即不完整。
3. 题3问危险时将做什么，条件对应change into a ball；题4Where used要用途处on everything from handbags to clothes，不是原from burrs灵感来源。
4. 第5横向列三对象的结构，再列各给人何产品。由共同special structures和共同help create helpful products得两个方面，逐一核都覆盖蜜袋鼯、犰狳和植物。
5. 回读五原答案的形式与题面：没限每空一词，第5两条分别完整；不把只某动物成立的飞行当全体共性。



## 例题

### 原例题 EN-TASK-E12

例1 (2024 河北中考)

阅读下面短文,按要求完成1\~5题。

Nature has many ways to solve problems. We humans learn a lot from animals and plants in nature. There is a science about how we copy great ideas from animals and plants to create helpful products. It is called biomimetics(仿生学).

Some animals such as this sugar glider have wing-like structures(结构) to help them fly from tree to tree. When they jump,

  
sugar glider

they spread out their arms and legs to help them fly down easily. Wingsuits, a kind of clothes, can let people experience flying. The idea of these clothes comes from the sugar glider.

  
armadillo

Some animals have structures used for self-protection. An armadillo has a hard cover that can protect it from other dangerous

animals. When an armadillo feels that it is in danger, it will change into a ball. Once the dangerous animal goes away, it comes out of the ball. The armadillo has taught people to create a backpack with a hard surface. The surface of the backpack protects things inside from being broken.

Plants have helped people come up with amazing inventions, too. Perhaps the best-known example is Velcro (尼龙搭扣). It includes two pieces of cloth. They stick to each other when they are pressed together. Velcro is used on everything from handbags to clothes. The idea of Velcro is from burrs (带芒刺的小果实) of a plant. They have special structures to allow themselves to stick to people's clothing or hair. They are taken to other places in this way.

From these examples, we can see biomimetics is widely used in our daily life. What other problems will nature help us solve in the future? Let's wait and see!

1、2 题完成句子；3\~5 题简略回答问题。

1. We humans learn a lot from \_\_\_\_.

2. The sugar gliders spread out \_\_\_\_ to help them fly down easily.

3. What will an armadillo do if it feels that it is in danger?

4. Where is Velcro used?

5. What do the kind of plant and two kinds of animals in the text have in common? (依据短文内容从两个不同的方面回答)

**原答案**：1.animals and plants in nature；2.their arms and legs；3.It will change into a ball.；4.Velcro is used on everything from handbags to clothes.；5.①They have special structures. ②They help people to create helpful products.。

**原解析（原文）**

【语篇解读】本文是一篇说明文,通过介绍人们仿照蜜袋鼯、犰狳及带刺植物的某些特征,发明一些有用的产品,让我们了解了仿生学。

1. 完成句子题。题干信息对应第一段第二句“We humans learn a lot from animals and plants in nature.”，由此可知我们人类从自然界的动植物身上学到了很多东西。故填“animals and plants in nature”。

2. 完成句子题。题干信息对应第二段第一、二句“Some animals such as this sugar glider...to help them fly down easily.”，由此可知蜜袋鼯张开胳膊和腿，帮助它们轻松地飞下来。故填“their arms and legs”。

3. 回答问题题(细节理解题)。由第三段第三句“When an armadillo feels that it is in danger, it will change into a ball.”可知,如果一只犰狳觉得自己处于危险之中,它就会变成一个球。故答案为“It will change into a ball.”。

4. 回答问题题(细节理解题)。由第四段中“Velcro is used on everything from handbags to clothes.”可知, 答案为“Velcro is used on everything from handbags to clothes.”

5. 回答问题题(归纳总结题)。由第二段的第一句、第三段的第一句及第四段的倒数第二句可知, 它们的共同点之一是都有特殊的结构。通读文章可知, 人们仿照蜜袋鼯的结构创造了跳伞服; 仿照犰狳创造了表面坚硬的背包; 仿照一种带芒刺的小果实创造了尼龙搭扣。综上可知, 它们的另一个共同点是都帮助人们创造有用的产品。故答案为“①They have special structures. ②They help people to create helpful products.”。

**完整解析（依据原解析展开）**

逐条先认任务。1原句主语We humans/谓语learn/宾语a lot/from来源，空在from后，抄来源animals and plants in nature，两类及in nature不漏。2谓语spread out后需张开的部位，原their arms and legs，复数sugar gliders→their不改成its。3条件danger定位armadillo change into a ball，原It will change into a ball.保存条件下行动；hard cover是结构非回答将做什么。4Where问使用处，第四段on everything from handbags to clothes列范围，原完整答保留；from burrs是发明灵感来源不答使用地点。5明确两个不同方面：三例均have special structures是一，help create helpful products是二，分别横向列蜜袋鼯→翼装、犰狳→硬包、果实→尼龙搭扣核每一对象。不能把飞行/自保当所有共有，也不能将hard cover与hard backpack写成两方面，它们只一对象的结构结果。题无最大词数，不套填表一词规则。

## 易错

按题型清单记名不够，真正分任务要看各小题指令。两个不同方面不是两个动物例，也不是一对象的结构与作用硬分成所有三者共有。
