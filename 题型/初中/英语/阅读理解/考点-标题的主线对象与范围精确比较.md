## 讲解

### 判据

best title先需全文主旨准确，再看标题覆盖和简洁；高频词只是找主线提示，不能以次数压过内容功能。

### 流程

1. 钱文依首尾和各段列来源、预算、目标储蓄、父母交流，核心是管理方法，B.Ways of Managing Money覆盖。
2. A原因、C目的、D储蓄条件各取偏面或改组织，不能全含money便等价。
3. 便条文从嘲笑午餐便条到全班23张便条感受，lunch note贯穿问题行动结局，C.Lunch Notes特指主线。
4. DIY Love太抽象，Good Friends只人际，Kind Offer泛善意；可相关却少故事关键物与过程。
5. 逐段反查候选是否容纳主要内容，准确后再取简洁醒目，不硬设标题必须几个词。
6. 原便条空白星号不为当前标题题补内容，完整原故事保留，不能借题型任务发明便条新句。



## 例题

### 原例题 EN-READ-E16

# 例 (2024 河北中考)

At an early age, we start learning how to manage money by watching our parents' spending habits. Don't worry if we haven't learned well how to save or spend money. The following life lessons about money can help us form good spending habits.

Money doesn't grow on trees. As kids, we don't often think about where money comes from. We need to know that our parents work hard to make money, and that it may be used up one day. Going to their workplaces to watch how they work is a great way to understand money is not that easy to make.

We shouldn't spend all our money in one go. It's important for us to have a budget (预算). In other words, we need to think about how much money we have and what we really want to spend it on. Realizing that our pocket money can't cover everything we want, we should make smart spending choices.

We should set a clear goal and watch our money grow. If we wish to own a new bicycle but can't afford it, we can save our pocket money. How? Write down how much money we have in a notebook and watch it grow. After we have enough, we can buy that wonderful bicycle we've dreamed about! Reaching our goal will greatly satiate us.

We can talk with our parents about how money is used and where money goes. Do you know what a bank account(账户) is? If not, communicate with our parents. We can also go to the supermarket with our parents to experience how money is spent in the real world.

Without doubt, we should treat money in a right way. Actually, learning about money isn't a hard job. From these lessons, we can get a sense of money management and right money values step by step!

What is the best title for the text?

A. Causes of Spending Money   
B. Ways of Managing Money   
C. Purposes of Treating Money   
D. Conditions of Saving Money

**原答案**：B。

**原解析（原文）**

通读全文尤其是本文的主题句(文章最后一句)“From these lessons, we can get a sense of money management and right money values step by step!”可知,本文主要谈论了青少年应该如何管理和使用零花钱,以及如何形成正确的消费观。B项“管理钱的方法”能概括本文大意。故选B。

**完整解析（依据原解析展开）**

总句life lessons关于花钱习惯，中间来源预算储蓄目标与父母交流，末money management，B管理钱方法覆盖全篇。A花钱原因仅一个方面且不是内容组织；C对待钱目的太偏目的，不统摄做法；D储蓄条件仅第三段范围。标题概括材料思想，不将具体零花钱建议变个人理财建议。

### 原例题 EN-READ-E17

# 例 (2024 江苏无锡中考)

Alex set his lunch tray on the table near his friend Leo. “Trade you some fries for some pretzels,” Alex said.

"Deal," said Leo, taking his bag of pretzels out of his lunchbox.

Alex noticed a folded piece of paper at the bottom of Leo's lunchbox.

“What’s that?” asked Daniel, another boy in their class.

Before Leo could answer, Daniel grabbed the paper. He unfolded it and read in a loud voice: "Dear Leo, I hope you have a great day. Love. Mom."

Daniel snorted, "Your mom puts notes in your lunchbox?"





"Yes," Leo said.

again, to himself. It almost looked as if he wanted to keep it.

Alex liked getting notes too.

Daniel tossed the note and it fell to the floor.

Alex picked it up. He handed it back to Leo, who put it into his pocket.

On the bus that afternoon, Alex thought about what had happened.

Alex's older brother, Mateo, arrived home just after Alex did. "How was your day, little dude?" Mateo asked.

"A boy in my class was mean to Leo," Alex said. He explained the way Daniel had acted.

Mateo shook his head. "How would Daniel like it if people picked on him for getting a note?"

“Daniel never gets notes,” Alex said. Suddenly, he had an idea. “That’s it! I know how to help Leo and Daniel.”

Alex explained his idea to Mateo. Mateo agreed to help, and they worked on the plan all afternoon.

What is the best title for this story?

A.DIY Love

B.Good Friends

C.Lunch Notes

D.Kind Offer

**原答案**：C。

**原解析（原文）**

根据文章第六段中“Your mom puts notes in your lunchbox?”、最后一段中“Now everyone knows how it feels to get a lunch note”以及文中反复出现的高频词“lunch”“note”可知，整篇文章主要围绕午餐便条展开。故选C。

**完整解析（依据原解析展开）**

整故事围绕母亲午餐便条被嘲、Alex想办法给全班23张便条、Daniel也喜爱便条，C.Lunch Notes为贯穿线索。A.DIY Love爱与制作太抽象，B.Good Friends仅人物关系不指出核心物，D.Kind Offer泛善意不能呈现故事特有主线。不是lunch出现多就够，便条承载问题行动结局才是标题。
