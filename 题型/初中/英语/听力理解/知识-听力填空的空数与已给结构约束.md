## 讲解

### 是什么

填空同时考信息与书写格式：需要什么内容、已有哪个词、还有几条空线、每空限几词。两词组成一个意义词块，不表示可以无条件塞进一条限一词的空。

### 怎么来的

护士原题每小题两条空线，每空限一词，所以one month、look after、on time、feel better、proud of分别一词填一空。OCR只留下每题一个占位符，若照抄文字会误认答案超词数；实际第47页确认是两空，原答案与规则不冲突。

田野表原Wear:sports____已给sports，空只补shoes；Leave school:at____o’clock已有at和o’clock，空只给nine/9。Don’t need:to bring tools or____已有tools，补另一午餐lunch。空前后词限定词性与内容，不能把听到完整sports shoes全抄造成重复。

keep yourself safe空要状态形容词，take medicine on time要副词性时间词块，make them feel better要使役后活动与评价。先核所听信息，再把它嵌回原结构检查是否缺介词或多了已给词；只凭词性合适也不够，仍须对应录音具体内容。

资料只给答案的组已明确注明无原详解，完整推理为据原文字稿的补写，不能伪称书上有该推导。

### 什么时候能用

先核真实题面空线与规则后填。扫描/OCR有疑点必须回原页，不能随意改“每空一词”或裁双词答案。实际任务允许数字与单词两种的nine/9均保留，不为单一格式删允许答案。

## 例题

### 原例题 EN-LISTEN-E14

例 听对话,用听到的单词完成下列句子,每空限填一词。

1. It's been about \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ since Alex's mum left home.

  
听力录音

2. Although nursing is not easy, Alex's mum likes to \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ patients.

3. Alex's mum needs to make sure her patients take medicine \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_.

4. Nurses sometimes talk with patients and their family members to make them \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_.

5. Alex is \_\_\_\_ \_\_\_\_ his mum because she helps save lives.

# 听力原文

M: I am so glad you are finally home, Mum.

W: I missed you, Alex. It's been about one month. I'm so happy to be back.

M: You look tired, Mum. I guess you've had hard days at work, haven't you?

W: Yes, I have. Nursing is no easy job. But I like to look after patients.

M: I guess you need to do a lot of things, like visiting patients, taking their temperature regularly, and making sure that they take medicine on time.

W: Right. Besides, I have other important duties.

M: You do?

W: Sure. Today we had several operations and we cleaned up the emergency room for a few hours. Sometimes we have a talk with patients, even their family members to make them feel better. And we tell patients how to work with doctors.

M: I didn't know that. I am proud of you, Mum. You have saved lives. How great!

W: Thank you, Alex. That's so sweet.

**OCR恢复说明**：实际PDF第47页每小题均两条空线，每空限一词；OCR合成一条，现恢复两空，原one month等双词答案分别填入。

**原答案**：1.one month；2.look after；3.on time；4.feel better；5.proud of。

原资料这一组只提供答案，未提供详解；下面保留原解答文字。

**原解析（原文）**

1.one month 2.look after 3.on time

4. feel better 5. proud of

**补写完整解析（据原文字稿逐项核对）**

第1组离家约one month，保留about，不是精确天数；第2组护士妈妈说like to look after patients，以look after填照看活动，原已有to不能重复to；第3组take medicine on time为按时服药，原temperature regularly属另任务不能移入此空；第4组make them feel better说明谈话让病人或家属好受些，make后用原形feel，不只填better漏动作；第5组Alex说I am proud of you，空接his mum用proud of，of属评价对象连接不能省。原5个答案均双词，需按原页核定两条空线及每空一词规则。

### 原例题 EN-LISTEN-E18

例（2024 河北中考）

听短文填空。



# Information Sheet Field Trip

Day: tomorrow

Pick: 1. \_\_\_\_

Leave school: 2. at \_\_\_\_ o’clock

Wear: 3. sports \_\_\_\_

Don’t need: 4. to bring tools or \_\_\_\_

Remember: 5. to keep yourself \_\_\_\_

# 听力原文

Hello, class. I'd like to tell you about the field trip tomorrow. We'll help the farmers pick apples on the hill behind our school. We can also learn about how to take care of apple trees. We'll leave school at 9 o'clock in the morning, so everybody should arrive 15 minutes earlier. We walk to the hill. You need to wear sports shoes. You don't need to bring any tools because our school will get them ready. Oh, our school will also offer us lunch, so you don't need to bring lunch, either. While working, you must follow the farmer's instructions. Please remember to keep yourself safe. We'll get back around 3 o'clock in the afternoon. Any questions?

**原解析（原文）**

**补写完整解析（据原文字稿逐项核对）**

## 易错

- 每题两词认原题错：OCR丢了一条空线。

- proud漏of：对象连接残缺。
- 原只有答案却声称详解原文完整：来源混淆。
